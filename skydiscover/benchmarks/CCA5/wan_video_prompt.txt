SCORING -- the score is a trace-normalized utility, NOT raw Mbps:
- Each trace's single-flow metrics are normalized against the best non-llmgen

- VIDEO utility uses target-rate style throughput saturation: reaching the target
  bitrate is rewarded (you can estimate the target bitrate based on baseline
  statistics), but excess throughput does not compensate for queueing delay or
  jitter.
- final score = application utility - fairness penalty - convergence penalty.

FAIRNESS (two concurrent QUIC flows on one bottleneck):
- Target Jain index >= 0.95 and a 40%-60% long-run throughput share.
- High single-flow throughput with Jain 0.5-0.8 counts as a FAILURE.
- Use local signals only: sustained queueing delay, rate plateau, losses with

CONVERGENCE:

- Slow convergence is penalized in proportion to convergence_time / duration.
